Bets

How bets are picked

A game gets flagged when it matches a rule that beat the closing line in past seasons. A flag becomes a bet only when that rule's backtest hit rate is at least 55% for college or 53.5% for NFL (52.4% breaks even at -110).

At most 5 bets a day, best hit rates first, one per game. Each day's budget is split by edge (fractional Kelly on a shrunk hit rate, $5 minimum), so the more proven rules get bigger stakes.

The rules and their records are on the Stats tab.

Using the page

Strike drops a bet. The rest of that day re-splits the budget and the next qualifying pick can move up. Undo brings it back. Striking a finished bet asks first, since it takes that result off your record.

Odds or stake: type over them on a bet. A stake you type is locked; clear the box to let it auto-size again. Bets freeze at kickoff.

Games: tap a game to type in a line, a QB change or the wind by hand. Typed values beat the automatic ones.

My own bets: tap + Add a bet, pick the game from ESPN's list for that day (every FBS and NFL game), then the bet, line, DraftKings odds and stake. They grade themselves from final scores and are tracked separately from the model, so the Tracker can compare the two. Enter the line as the number on your ticket for the team you picked (e.g. -3.5), or the total for over/under.

Games list: Flagged shows only games that match a rule; With lines shows every game that has a line; All shows everything.

Reading the Tracker

Model bets is the money record of the bets the model chose. My own bets is the record of bets you added yourself. The box at the top compares the two with the same filters. Every pick grades every flag the model made, bet or not, to show which rules are really working.

On the win-rate chart the dashed line is the 52.4% break-even; gray bars have fewer than 5 settled bets, too few to read much into. Pick what to split by with Slice by.

Budget

Rules: CFB 55%+, NFL 53.5%+, top 5 bets per day, one per game, stakes split by edge (fractional Kelly), $5 minimum.

Sync with GitHub

Strikes, edited odds and stakes, typed-in lines, your own bets and the bets this page makes are saved to state.json on the sync branch of your betting-model repository, so your phone and PC always match and nothing is lost if browser data is cleared. Every device reads it on open. To save changes, a device needs a GitHub key that can write only to betting-model (Contents: Read and write). The key stays on this device.

Save your changes to the official log

Only needed if GitHub sync is off. Strikes, edited stakes and odds, and bets the page made are stored in this browser. Save them as page_changes.json into Betting Model\betting_models\logs. The Tuesday-morning refresh merges them into the log so every device and the weekly report see them.
