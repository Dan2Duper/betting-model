How bets are picked

A game gets flagged when it matches a rule that beat the closing line in past seasons. A flag becomes a bet only when that rule's backtest hit rate is at least 55% for college or 53.5% for NFL (52.4% breaks even at -110).

At most 5 bets a day, best hit rates first, one per game. Each day's budget is split by edge (fractional Kelly on a shrunk hit rate, $5 minimum), so the more proven rules get bigger stakes.

Current bet rules (backtested against closing lines, Oct 2026 review): NFL wind unders, NFL 2-team underdog teasers (+1.5/+2.5 teased to +7.5/+8.5 when the total is 49 or less, paired automatically at DraftKings' −120), CFB east team flying west, CFB wind unders, CFB totals of 68+ under, CFB road team off a 28+ point loss, SEC/Big Ten road teams off a 21+ loss or 3-game skid (half stakes), and CFB letdown fades (half stakes). Leans are tracked but not bet.

Kill switch: after 50 live bets a rule is retired if it falls below 52.4% (teaser legs below 73.9%); a lean is promoted after 50 live picks at 55% or better. Records are on the Stats tab.

Using the page

AI-LOX are the model's picks, with suggested stakes from the daily budget. They are tracked as the model's record whether or not you bet them. A bet only counts as yours when it's in My bets (added on the Week tab or sent to Claude as a DraftKings screenshot). If one of your bets matches an AI-LOX (same game, market and side) it's marked and counted under My AI-LOX too.

Games: tap a game to type in a line, a QB change or the wind by hand. Typed values beat the automatic ones.

My bets: tap + Add a bet, pick the game from ESPN's list for that day (every FBS and NFL game), then the bet, line, DraftKings odds and stake. They grade themselves from final scores and are tracked separately from the model, so the Tracker can compare the two. Enter the line as the number on your ticket for the team you picked (e.g. -3.5), or the total for over/under.

Model games: Major (the default) shows every college game with a top-60 team by Elo rank (the #number by each team) or a Power 4 team (SEC, Big Ten, Big 12, ACC) or Notre Dame, and every NFL game. Every FBS-vs-FBS game is modeled; games against FCS teams are not. The Scores tab shows the model's score and lean on each modeled game. Flagged shows only games that match a rule; With lines shows every game that has a line; All shows everything.

Reading the Tracker

My bets is your real money record. My AI-LOX is the part of it that matched the model's locks. All AI-LOX is the model's record at suggested stakes, whether or not you bet them. All flags grades every flag the model made, bet or not, to show which rules are really working.

On the win-rate chart the dashed line is the 52.4% break-even; gray bars have fewer than 5 settled bets, too few to read much into. Pick what to split by with Slice by.

Sync with GitHub

Your bets, typed-in lines, saved pre-kick lines and the AI-LOX this page makes are saved to state.json on the sync branch of your betting-model repository, so your phone and PC always match and nothing is lost if browser data is cleared. Every device reads it on open. To save changes, a device needs a GitHub key that can write only to betting-model (Contents: Read and write). The key stays on this device.

Save your changes to the official log

Only needed if GitHub sync is off. Your bets and the AI-LOX the page made are stored in this browser. Save them as page_changes.json into Betting Model\betting_models\logs. The Tuesday-morning refresh merges them into the log so every device and the weekly report see them.
